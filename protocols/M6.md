# M6 — tasks with variants (registered 2026-11-04, before the first run)

**Question.** When the agent must pick exact variants (size, dimensions), build several lines and stay within a budget on the delivered total including shipping, does KaliCart give more verified totals and fewer calls?

**Tasks.**
- V1, store A: two black briefs of the same model in sizes 2 and 3, plus a black bra.
- V2, store A: one black brief in size 3, budget below the free-shipping threshold (shipping counts).
- V3, store B (mattresses): a single mattress 80x190 cm and two identical pillows.
- V4, store B: a double mattress 160x190 cm and a topper of the same size.

**Runs.** 20 per arm (5 per task). Model `deepseek-flash`, default temperature. Feasibility of every task checked with a real quote before registration.

**Thresholds.**
- A. Verified K >= 16/20 and K - S >= 4.
- B. Median calls of K <= 60% of S.

**Deviations.**
1. After 2 of 40 runs, before any S run: the scorer recognised the product type from its name only and rejected a padded triangle bra. Fixed to use name plus store categories; the two runs were rescored.
2. The second half was moved forward by a few hours, above the self-imposed cap of 10 runs per store per day; a 60 s pause was added between runs.

**Verdict.** A: no (20/20 vs 20/20). B: no (median 10.5 vs 14 calls, 25% fewer). Tokens, descriptive: median 281k vs 533k, 47% fewer; time 37 s vs 65 s. K's 23 failed calls are all 429 from the KaliCart catalog rate limiter, in two runs at the end of a day of traffic from one IP; the harness was dropping the Retry-After header (fixed for M7). S's 24 failed calls are agent errors on the cart, mostly 401 for a missing nonce.
