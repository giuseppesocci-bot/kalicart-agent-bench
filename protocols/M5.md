# M5 — simple products (registered 2026-11-03, before the first run)

**Question.** With the same agent, does the KaliCart path give more verified delivered totals and fewer calls than the WooCommerce Store API?

**Tasks** (one cart of two products each, budget on the delivered total):
- N1, store A (lingerie): one dark brief and one dark bra, any available size.
- N2, store C (popcorn supplies): one popcorn corn and one popcorn oil.
- N3, store D (farm food): one jam and one extra-virgin olive oil.

**Runs.** 15 per arm (5 per task), fresh session each, alternating K and S. Agent: the one-tool harness in `harness/`, model DeepSeek `deepseek-flash` via API, temperature 0.

**Thresholds.**
- A. Verified K >= 12/15 and K - S >= 3.
- B. Median calls of K <= 60% of S.

**Deviations.**
1. The N2 budget was not reachable (the cheapest oil cost more than the whole budget). Budget raised; the two runs made before the change were excluded and kept.
2. One S run was stopped by the harness cost cap, whose price estimate was about three times too high. Counted as not verified; the verdict is the same however it is counted.

Five earlier runs were made by hand with the same model in a desktop agent app; they are a pilot and are not part of these numbers.

**Verdict.** A: no (K 14/15, S 12/15). B: no (median 8 vs 10 calls, 20% fewer). Tokens, not a registered measure: median 107k vs 373k, 71% fewer. On store C, which sells only simple products, S needed slightly fewer calls than K (8 vs 9).
